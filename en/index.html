BEFORE YOU START

What you need
to connect.

Connect to the Redmine your team already uses. If you’re unsure whether its REST API is enabled, check with your Redmine administrator.

  • Redmine 6.0 / 6.1 / 7.0One of these supported versions.
  • HTTPS and the REST APIYour iPhone must be able to reach Redmine over HTTPS, with its REST API enabled.
  • Your Redmine URL and API keyUse the address you normally open in your browser. To get your API key, open Redmine from the app’s connection screen.

Depending on your Redmine settings, you may be unable to connect or use some features.

Connect. Then open your first task.

  1. Enter your Redmine URLOpen the app and enter the address you normally use for Redmine.
