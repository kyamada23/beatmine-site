Privacy policy

How BeatMine handles, stores, and transfers information.

The developer of BeatMine for Redmine (“the app”) does not collect any information about you.

Information the app handles

The app stores the Redmine URL and API key you enter, your comment drafts, records used to check whether updates arrived, your project filter (project numbers) and a record of tasks you opened (task numbers and update times). These are stored only in the device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain). The app also stores, within the app, a mark showing whether you have seen the swipe hint and a record used to decide when to ask for an App Store rating (the number of completions, the date of your first completion, the last date a send failed or its result was unknown, and the last date a rating was requested together with the app version at that time). This record contains no task content or connection details and is never sent to the developer or any third party. Your task list, task descriptions, attachments, completions and comments are sent and received directly between the device and your Redmine. Task descriptions, attached images and earlier comments are loaded only to display them and are not stored on the device. The developer runs no server, and this information is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not analyze usage, show ads, track you or collect crash reports (except information Apple collects according to your device settings).

Deletion

“Delete connection” in “Settings” removes everything stored in the Keychain from the device. The swipe hint mark and the rating record are removed when you delete the app. If you delete and reinstall the app, any earlier information is deleted rather than reused.

What you send to Redmine

Completions and comments are recorded in your Redmine. How they are handled is governed by the policies of the organization that runs your Redmine.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
