The developer of BeatMine for Redmine (“the app”) does not collect any information about you.

Information the app handles

The app stores the Redmine URL and API key you enter, your comment drafts, and records used to check whether updates arrived. These are stored only in the device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain). Your task list, completions and comments are sent and received directly between the device and your Redmine. The developer runs no server, and this information is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not analyze usage, show ads, track you or collect crash reports (except information Apple collects according to your device settings).

Deletion

“Delete connection” in “Settings” removes all of the above from the device. If you delete and reinstall the app, any earlier information is deleted rather than reused.

What you send to Redmine

Completions and comments are recorded in your Redmine. How they are handled is governed by the policies of the organization that runs your Redmine.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
